# Source Corpus

Status: Approved for story development on 2026-08-08

This corpus distinguishes ancient testimony from modern orientation. Ancient works conflict; a citation proves that a version existed, not that it was the single Greek canon. Section and line references remain authoritative if a hosting URL changes.

## Working source policy

- Use `Heracles` when discussing Greek testimony and `Hercules` for the film title and screenplay-facing English name.
- Label material as `ancient source`, `later synthesis`, `inference`, or `original invention` in downstream story documents.
- Cite passage numbers, not only URLs.
- Do not infer visual design from a literary epithet alone.
- Do not treat Roman-era summaries as evidence that every detail was present in archaic Greece.
