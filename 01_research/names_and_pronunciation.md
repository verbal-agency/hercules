# Names and Pronunciation

Status: Approved working pronunciation sheet on 2026-08-08

Pronunciations are accessible English approximations, not a claim to reconstruct one ancient Greek dialect. Stress is shown in capitals.

## Naming rule

- Screenplay/title: **Hercules**.
- Research discussion of Greek sources: **Heracles**.
- Use Greek names for the surrounding pantheon and world: Zeus, Hera, Athena, Artemis, Hermes, Hades, Persephone—not Jupiter, Juno, Minerva, Diana, Mercury, Pluto, or Proserpina.
- Preserve quoted source spellings in quotations and bibliography metadata.

## Dialogue guidance

- Do not alternate `Hercules` and `Heracles` as though they are two people.
- Avoid explanatory dialogue about etymology unless it becomes dramatically necessary in G003.
- Actors may use a consistent natural English accent system; no “generic ancient” accent is prescribed by this sheet.
