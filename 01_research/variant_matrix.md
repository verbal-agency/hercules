# Episode and Variant Matrix

Status: Approved for treatment development on 2026-08-08; amended by AD-025, AD-026, AD-028, AD-030, and AD-031

`Selected` means binding for this adaptation. It does not claim historical priority.

## Chronology and frame

| Topic | Ancient variants | Selected | Rejected or reserved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Madness | Before the Labors in Pseudo-Apollodorus 2.4.12; after the Labors in Euripides, *Heracles* | Madness precedes service, so the Labors are externally imposed penance that Hercules gradually internalizes as atonement | Euripides's placement is rejected for chronology but retained as a source for aftermath, shame, and Theseus's friendship |
| Cause of service | Purification, Delphi, and Eurystheus in Pseudo-Apollodorus; broader destiny/civilizing mission in Diodorus | Delphi orders Hercules to serve Eurystheus; Hera exploited the birth order that made Eurystheus king | A simple “quest for glory” motive is rejected |
| Number | Ten tasks; Hydra and Stables disallowed, producing two additional tasks in Pseudo-Apollodorus | Ten announced, twelve completed | A pre-existing clean list of twelve is rejected because the disputed count dramatizes power and unfairness |
| Order | Lists vary; Olympia's metopes use twelve without fixing a narrative itinerary | Pseudo-Apollodorus order remains the underlying causal index, but G003 may use chaptered nonlinear presentation, flashforward, delayed context, distant intercutting, and anticipatory character/creature scenes; story time and presentation order must be tracked separately | Treating vase/metope arrangement as a chronological itinerary is rejected; nonlinear display may not break the ten-to-twelve causality or the progression of Hercules's choices |

## The twelve Labors

| # | Labor | Selected narrative version | Material alternatives rejected or reserved | Sources |
|---:|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nemean Lion | Hercules discovers weapons cannot pierce the hide, wrestles and strangles the lion, then adopts its skin; Eurystheus responds with fear rather than admiration | Killing it with sword or club; extended Molorchus hospitality is optional background, not a required beat | SRC_A01 2.5.1; SRC_A02 4.11.3–4; Met REF_0001 |
| 2 | Lernaean Hydra | Iolaus cauterizes the necks while Hercules removes the heads; Hercules buries the immortal head and preserves venom. Eurystheus rejects the Labor because Hercules accepted help | A fully solitary victory; Hera's crab as more than a brief hostile complication; graphic venom harvesting | SRC_A01 2.5.2; SRC_A02 4.11.5–6 |
| 3 | Ceryneian Hind | A year-long living capture tests patience; Hercules answers Artemis that necessity, not contempt, drove him | Killing the hind; resolving the chase mainly through an arrow wound; confusing it with an ordinary deer | SRC_A01 2.5.3; SRC_A02 4.13.1 |
| 4 | Erymanthian Boar | Hercules drives the boar into deep snow and brings it alive; Eurystheus's fear supplies restrained humor | The fatal centaur/Chiron chain is reserved as off-spine mythology because it overwhelms the Labor; making Eurystheus a clown | SRC_A01 2.5.4; SRC_A02 4.12.1–2 |
| 5 | Augean Stables | Hercules diverts rivers and solves the task through engineering; Augeas's promised payment lets Eurystheus invalidate the result as “for hire” | Personally shoveling the entire stable; resolving the later war with Augeas inside this film | SRC_A01 2.5.5; SRC_A02 4.13.3 |
| 6 | Stymphalian Birds | Athena supplies bronze clappers made by Hephaestus; sound flushes the birds and Hercules drives them away/downs only those necessary | Treating every bird as a metallic robot; mass slaughter as the point; omitting Athena's practical aid | SRC_A01 2.5.6; SRC_A02 4.13.2 |
| 7 | Cretan Bull | Hercules refuses Minos's offered aid, captures the bull alive, presents it, and it is released | Identifying it definitively as Europa's bull; resolving Theseus's later Marathonian Bull story here | SRC_A01 2.5.7; SRC_A02 4.13.4 |
| 8 | Mares of Diomedes | Diomedes's cruelty has made the mares man-eating; Hercules defeats him and calms/captures the team. Abderus's death is shown clearly as a cost of command without turning him into spectacle | Feeding Diomedes to the mares as spectacle; an anonymous solo raid that removes responsibility for companions | SRC_A01 2.5.8; SRC_A02 4.15.3–4; SRC_A11 *Fabula* 30; AD-024 |
| 9 | Belt of Hippolyta | Hippolyta initially agrees to give the belt. Hera spreads a kidnapping lie; mutual mistrust triggers battle and Hippolyta's death, which Hercules recognizes as a failure of judgment | Amazons attacked without provocation; Hippolyta reduced to a prize; Theseus abducting Antiope during this Labor | SRC_A01 2.5.9; contrast SRC_A02 4.16 |
| 10 | Cattle of Geryon | Far-west journey, solar bowl, Orthrus, herdsman, and three-bodied Geryon; Hercules must shepherd the living cattle home through Hera's disruption | A colonial “civilizing natives” travelogue; every roadside combat in the long return; triple-bodied gore | SRC_A01 2.5.10; SRC_A02 4.17–25 |
| 11 | Apples of the Hesperides | Hercules frees Prometheus, learns the route, bears the sky while Atlas retrieves the apples, and uses judgment to make Atlas resume his burden; Athena later restores the apples | Hercules simply killing Ladon and taking the fruit; locating the garden on a realistic map; making Atlas merely foolish | SRC_A01 2.5.11; SRC_A08 *Olympian* 3.11–45 |
| 12 | Cerberus | After initiation and permission, Hercules descends unarmed, frees Theseus but cannot free Pirithous, subdues Cerberus without weapons, shows him to Eurystheus, and returns him unharmed | Killing or permanently stealing Cerberus; defeating Hades; rescuing Pirithous; ending with apotheosis | SRC_A01 2.5.12; SRC_A12 30–35 |

## Connected episodes

| Episode | Decision | Reason and trace |
|---|---|---|
| Birth-order trick | Include in compressed form | Explains Eurystheus's legal power and Hera's indirect methods; SRC_A01 2.4.5, SRC_A05 19.95–133 |
| Infant serpents | Include in compressed early dramatization and recall through later Olympian gossip as part of Hera's continuous campaign | Establishes Hera, Athena, impossible strength, and the gods' long tolerance of the persecution; SRC_A01 2.4.8, SRC_A09, REF_0002; AD-028 |
| Linus's death | Mention or imply as evidence of uncontrolled anger; do not dramatize unless G003 proves necessary | SRC_A01 2.4.9; prevents madness from feeling like his first dangerous episode |
| Minyan war and marriage to Megara | Include as pre-madness context | SRC_A01 2.4.11–12; anchors home, honor, and loss |
| Alcestis | Omit | Powerful but belongs to a different moral episode and duplicates return-from-death imagery |
| Argonauts | Omit from the feature spine | Chronology and participation vary; no causal need for the atonement story |
| Antaeus, Busiris, Cacus, and other travel exploits | Reserve as compression options within journeys, not independent required beats | Avoids an episodic catalog |
| Omphale | Omit/reserve for another story | Occurs after Iphitus and a later servitude; outside the selected ending |
| Prometheus | Show chained and suffering on his rock before Labor 11, then pay off his encounter and release within Labor 11 | Unifies the divine world, reveals the violence of Olympian order, and lets Hercules use strength to release rather than dominate; SRC_A01 2.5.11; AD-030 |
| Theseus in Hades | Include within Labor 12 | Pays off human solidarity without importing the chronology of Euripides wholesale |
| Deianeira/Nessus/Iole/pyre/apotheosis | Reserve for sequel; no dramatized coda | Protects the first film's atonement ending and preserves B001 |
| Olympian frame | Include recurring Olympus scenes in which Hera plots and the gods gossip, debate, aid, or abstain | Makes divine causation an active current through mortal events and reveals the persecution progressively rather than through one exposition scene; original framing grounded in SRC_A03 and SRC_A05; AD-026–AD-028 |
| Hercules's haunting | Include evolving nightmares and waking intrusions after the family deaths | Keeps the irreparable act present across the Labors without resurrecting Megara as a forgiving ghost; original device influenced by SRC_A03; AD-025 |
