## Story canon

### AD-001 — Madness precedes the Labors

- Decision: Use Pseudo-Apollodorus's chronology. Hera causes a catastrophic break after Hercules has built a home with Megara; the Labors follow as legal/religious purification and become chosen atonement.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.12. Euripides's post-Labor chronology (SRC_A03) is documented but not combined with it.
- Rationale: This creates the feature's causal spine and makes strength morally insufficient.

### AD-002 — The dead are not reversible plot tokens

- Decision: Megara and the children die. No resurrection, secret survival, or revelation that the deaths were an illusion.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.12 and SRC_A03.
- Rationale: Atonement requires an irreparable loss. The film must not imply that completing tasks purchases forgiveness from the dead.

### AD-003 — Responsibility is shared but not erased

- Decision: Hera deliberately precipitates the madness; Hercules is not morally guilty in the ordinary intentional sense, yet he accepts responsibility for living after harm done through his body and power.
- Rationale: Avoid both victim-blaming and a consequence-free possession story.

### AD-004 — Ten tasks become twelve

- Decision: Eurystheus announces ten, then rejects the Hydra because of Iolaus's help and the Stables because of promised payment.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.5.1–12.
- Rationale: The shifting count embodies institutional cruelty and tests whether Hercules serves only for acquittal.

### AD-005 — The Labor arc moves from force toward restraint

- Decision: G003 must organize the selected Labor material so Hercules increasingly solves problems through patience, cooperation, mercy, ingenuity, and endurance.
- Classification: Original dramatic organization grounded in ancient episodes.
- Rationale: Repetition becomes character progression rather than twelve interchangeable victories.

### AD-006 — Completion is not absolution

- Decision: Returning Cerberus ends compulsory service, not grief. Hercules's climax is choosing continued life and relational responsibility.
- Basis: Original resolution influenced by Theseus stopping suicide in SRC_A03 1214–1428.

### AD-007 — The first feature ends after Cerberus

- Decision: End with service complete, Cerberus restored alive, and Hercules stepping into a self-directed life. Deianeira, Nessus, Iole, the poisoned robe, pyre, and apotheosis are neither dramatized nor teased in a mandatory coda.
- Basis: The reserved cycle is SRC_A01 2.7.5–7, SRC_A04, and SRC_A02 4.36–39.
- Rationale: Preserves a complete atonement film and protects a later tragedy from summary treatment.

## Character functions

### AD-008 — Megara is the human center of the lost life

- Decision: Megara is Hercules's adult partner and moral equal, not a reward or disposable motivation. Her agency, family relationships, and perception of Hercules must be established before the catastrophe. She does not appear as a recurring ghost who forgives him.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.11–12; the expanded threatened family in SRC_A03.

### AD-009 — Iolaus makes interdependence unavoidable

- Decision: Iolaus is Hercules's nephew, companion, driver, and practical counterweight. His indispensable Hydra aid is real even though Eurystheus weaponizes it to reject the Labor.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.11, 2.5.2.

### AD-010 — Eurystheus is frightened power, not comic relief

- Decision: Eurystheus is a legitimate king produced by Hera's birth-order intervention. He is fearful, status-conscious, and procedurally cruel; brief vessel-hiding humor is allowed, but he remains dangerous.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.5, 2.5.1; SRC_A05 19.95–133.

### AD-011 — Hera is the divine antagonist, not a wronged-wife caricature

- Decision: Hera targets Zeus's visible exception to divine and civic order. Her persecution is intentional and unjust; the film does not excuse it as ordinary jealousy or make Alcmene/Megara responsible for Zeus's acts.
- Basis: SRC_A01, SRC_A05, SRC_A09.

### AD-012 — Athena aids without solving

- Decision: Athena supplies bounded tools, knowledge, or openings—especially the Stymphalian clappers—and may protect the infant, but Hercules and companions must choose and act.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.8, 2.5.6; SRC_A05 8.362–69; REF_0002.

### AD-013 — Theseus embodies survival through solidarity

- Decision: Hercules frees Theseus during the Cerberus descent. Their relationship, seeded no later than G003, lets Theseus recognize and answer Hercules's isolation. Pirithous remains bound.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.5.12, SRC_A12; thematic influence from SRC_A03.

### AD-014 — Zeus is absent power with consequences

- Decision: Zeus does not descend to settle the conflict or apologize. His past actions create Hercules's predicament; his silence keeps the human cost visible.
- Basis: Ancient traditions consistently establish paternity but differ on divine involvement.
- Current status: Superseded by AD-027; retained as decision history.

### AD-015 — The gods use bounded manifestations

- Decision: Hera, Athena, Hermes, Artemis, Apollo, Hades, and Persephone may appear when selected episodes require them. Divine action must have legible rules and cannot reverse the central loss.
- Rationale: Keeps mythic scale without repeated deus ex machina.
- Current status: Superseded in scope by AD-026; the bounded-action rule remains in force.

## Tone and representation

### AD-016 — Audience boundary

- Decision: Aim for a family-accessible teen audience, approximately a modern PG-13 boundary: emotionally serious, frightening at times, and intelligible to younger viewers with adults, but never graphic.
- Violence rules: no visible killing of children; no lingering bodies; no gore; creature combat may be intense but prioritizes silhouette, consequence, and reaction; sexual violence is not depicted; suicide ideation may be expressed but not instructional or romanticized.
- Humor rules: humor releases pressure through character, scale, effort, and Eurystheus's fear. It may not mock grief, disability, foreign peoples, Amazons, or the victims of violence.
- Rationale: Resolves the carried G002 audience risk consistently with `PROJECT.md`.
- Current status: Superseded by AD-024; retained as decision history.

### AD-017 — Madness is subjective and non-diagnostic

- Decision: Depict the event as divinely induced perceptual rupture, not as a named real-world mental illness. Avoid “madness equals evil,” asylum imagery, and stigmatizing shorthand.
- Basis: Personified Lyssa in SRC_A03 and Hera's agency in SRC_A01.

### AD-018 — Hippolyta's tragedy is caused by disinformation

- Decision: Hippolyta initially consents to give the belt. Hera's false report and Hercules's readiness to perceive betrayal turn agreement into fatal conflict.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.5.9.
- Rationale: Gives the Amazon episode moral consequence and avoids conquest-as-default.

### AD-019 — Cultural and visual period boundary

- Decision: The story world is mythic Greek rather than a literal archaeological year. G004 may synthesize Late Bronze Age massing and technologies with Archaic/Classical iconographic clarity, but must not use Roman imperial Hercules as the unmarked default.
- Rules: Greek social vocabulary and names govern; no pop-medieval fantasy; no claim of archaeological reconstruction; geographic cultures must not become interchangeable “barbarian” caricatures.

### AD-020 — Naming policy

- Decision: The film title and English dialogue use `Hercules`; research uses `Heracles` for Greek-source precision. Greek forms govern other names (`Megara`, `Iolaus`, `Eurystheus`, `Athena`, `Heracles` in quoted scholarship), with familiar English spellings.
- Rationale: Audience recognition without silently Romanizing the whole world.

## Invention policy

### AD-021 — Permitted invention

- Decision: Original connective events, dialogue, relationship development, geography compression, and Labor intercutting are allowed when labeled in treatment notes and consistent with approved outcomes.
- Required test: invention must clarify causality, character, or production feasibility and may not be presented as ancient testimony.

### AD-022 — Prohibited flattening

- Decision: Do not merge contradictory variants merely to accumulate famous details. Select one outcome, record alternatives, and identify thematic borrowing across variants.

### AD-023 — Labor compression

- Decision: All twelve Labors exist in story canon, but G003 may allocate full sequences, short sequences, montage treatment, or off-screen completion. Each must retain its selected outcome and contribution to the character arc.
- Rationale: A 105–115 minute feature cannot give twelve equal set pieces and still sustain drama.

## Post-approval amendments

### AD-024 — Adult rating and visible consequence

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: AD-016.
- Decision: Target an R rating. Violence is shown rather than systematically displaced offscreen, including the catastrophic family event and the mortal cost of later Labors. The claymation medium may stylize wounds through fingerprints, deformation, torn material, pigment, and replacement pieces, but style must not erase pain or automatically be assumed to reduce the rating.
- Boundary: Every violent image must serve character, consequence, theme, or plot. The treatment will be tasteful and never gratuitous. The family killings must be unmistakable but may use fractured point of view; children and bodies are not displayed for spectacle. Creature and human injury may be graphic enough to carry consequence, but sexual violence, sadistic dwelling, and humor at a victim's expense remain prohibited.
- Evidence/classification: User-directed original tone choice; ancient tragic consequence in SRC_A03 informs emotional seriousness but not a modern rating.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 treatment and scene index; G004 tone, violence, memory, and material rules; G005 screenplay; G006 damage-state assets; G007–G010 shot, generation, and editorial packages.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-025 — Hercules is haunted through evolving nightmares

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: None; extends AD-002, AD-003, AD-008, and AD-017.
- Decision: Recurrent nightmares, involuntary sensory memories, and waking intrusions make the deaths dramatically present throughout the feature. Each recurrence must change with Hercules: first perceptual rupture and denial, then accusatory repetition, then contamination by new failures, and finally an ability to remember without mistaking endurance for absolution.
- Boundary: Megara and the children do not return as autonomous ghosts and never grant forgiveness. Hera may exploit the imagery, but the film must distinguish divine intrusion from Hercules's own memory wherever the distinction matters to a choice.
- Evidence/classification: Original subjective device grounded in the shame, suicide crisis, and survival themes of SRC_A03.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 character arc and beat sheet; G004 nightmare grammar; G005 screenplay; G006 memory-state derivatives; G007 shot continuity.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-026 — Olympus interpenetrates the mortal world

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: AD-015's restriction of gods primarily to episode-bound manifestations.
- Decision: The feature includes recurring scenes on Olympus. Hera plans and reacts there; other gods observe, gossip, form factions, withhold aid, or offer bounded help. Divine choices cross into mortal life through signs, rumors, tools, punishments, institutions, and direct manifestations, while mortal outcomes provoke new alignments and actions among the gods. Every Olympus scene must either alter pressure on the mortal plot, expose a consequential piece of divine history, or change the audience's understanding of the Labors.
- Boundary: Olympus is an interpenetrating dramatic court and damaged family system, not an exposition balcony or detachable parallel story. Divine characters may know more than mortals but do not narrate every outcome in advance. Direct intervention remains bounded and cannot reverse the central loss.
- Evidence/classification: Original framing device drawing on divine conflicts and interventions in SRC_A03 and SRC_A05.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 structure and divine character arcs; G004 Olympus visual grammar; G005 screenplay; G006 divine-character and Olympus assets.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-027 — Zeus is present and culpably passive

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: AD-014.
- Decision: Zeus appears in Olympus scenes but does not descend to rescue Hercules or erase Hera's actions. He protects his authority and the stability of divine decrees while refusing the personal cost of openly defending his son. The film treats this inaction as a choice with consequences, not as neutral cosmic necessity.
- Boundary: Zeus does not settle the plot, apologize the harm away, or become a benign father whose hidden plan excuses suffering.
- Evidence/classification: Original characterization grounded in Zeus's paternity and Hera's divine opposition across SRC_A01, SRC_A03, and SRC_A05.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 divine ensemble and Olympus scenes; G004 divine material hierarchy; G005 screenplay; G006 Zeus asset set.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-028 — Hera's sabotage is one continuous campaign

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: None; expands AD-011 and the connected-episode selections.
- Decision: Hera's birth-order intervention, the cradle serpents, the perceptual rupture, Eurystheus's authority, the Amazon rumor, and later disruptions are presented or recalled as stages of one campaign. The gods gossip about this pattern from Hercules's cradle onward, revealing how long Hera has been testing the limits of what Olympus will tolerate.
- Strategy: Hera exploits existing monsters, laws, fears, rumors, and divine bargains rather than secretly manufacturing every obstacle. She wants Hercules dead, discredited, or remade into proof that Zeus's mortal exception is a catastrophe. His moral growth is the outcome she fails to anticipate.
- Evidence/classification: SRC_A01 2.4.5–2.5.12, SRC_A05 19.95–133, and SRC_A09 establish repeated persecution; the single continuous strategy and Olympian gossip are original synthesis.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 causal structure and Hera arc; G004 recurring divine motifs; G005 screenplay; G006 Hera continuity states and sabotage props/effects.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-029 — One world, not twelve disconnected adventures

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: None; extends AD-005, AD-019, AD-021, and AD-023.
- Decision: G003 must connect the Labors through persistent geography, political consequences, travelers and messengers, changing public reputation, recurring divine actors, retained tools and wounds, and setup/payoff across destinations. Olympus, Thebes, Tiryns, the edges of the world, Prometheus's rock, Atlas's burden, and Hades are regions of one mythic order.
- Boundary: Compression is allowed, but a Labor cannot feel like a reset. Physical, relational, moral, or political state must cross every transition.
- Evidence/classification: Original continuity principle using the connected itinerary and consequences of SRC_A01 and SRC_A02.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 treatment and scene index; G004 color/geographic continuity; G005 screenplay; G006 location and state assets; G007 inheritance contracts.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-030 — The Labors carry several purposes, revealed in stages

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: None; clarifies AD-001, AD-004, AD-005, and AD-006.
- Decision: The feature progressively reveals five simultaneous readings of the Labors: Delphi's legal-religious purification, Eurystheus's political containment, Hera's attempted destruction or disgrace of Hercules, the world's need to survive predatory powers, and Hercules's self-chosen practice of responsibility. No single speech provides the whole answer.
- Structure rule: G003 must plant and revise these readings at multiple points—before service, after an early invalidation, near the midpoint, when ten becomes twelve, and at Cerberus—so the audience keeps reassessing whether the Labors punish, exploit, transform, or free him.
- Prometheus rule: Prometheus must be seen chained and suffering on his rock before Labor 11, then encountered and freed during that Labor. His recurring punishment makes divine order visible and turns Hercules's strength from domination toward release.
- Evidence/classification: Purification and service derive from SRC_A01 2.4.12; political precedence from SRC_A05 19.95–133; civilizing purpose from SRC_A02; Prometheus from SRC_A01 2.5.11; the staged synthesis is original.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 story principles, treatment, beat sheet, and scene index; G004 Prometheus/Olympus imagery; G005 screenplay; G006 Prometheus and rock assets.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-031 — Braided, selectively nonlinear presentation

- Date: 2026-08-08.
- Supersedes: None; clarifies AD-005, AD-025, AD-026, AD-029, and AD-030.
- Decision: Hercules remains the protagonist, but he does not monopolize sequence agency or introduce every Labor. The presentation may jump in story time, intercut distant regions, withhold causes, show consequences before their explanation, and open movements from Hera's, Eurystheus's, Iolaus's, a threatened community's, Prometheus's, Atlas's, Cerberus's, or another consequential figure's position. Supporting characters and the world must be doing things that alter the plot before Hercules arrives.
- Chronology rule: The underlying causal chronology remains binding—madness precedes service, early choices accumulate into later change, ten tasks become twelve through the two invalidations, Prometheus precedes Atlas within Labor 11, and Cerberus completes service—but the audience need not receive those events in simple chronological order. Every displaced scene must create suspense, dramatic irony, thematic collision, or later recontextualization rather than exist solely as a puzzle flourish.
- Transition rule: Do not default to a loop of return to court, assignment, Labor, homecoming, and nightmare. Assignments and future dangers may be revealed through Hera's plots, Eurystheus's decisions, divine gossip, omens, victims, creatures, geography, or anticipatory glimpses of Prometheus, Atlas, Cerberus, and other figures. Scene-to-scene momentum should normally come from an outgoing decision, consequence, image, sound, object, or unanswered question.
- Haunting rule: A nightmare is never obligatory after a Labor. Memory and divine intrusion should invade ongoing travel, combat, intimacy, or decision-making only when the intrusion changes Hercules's behavior or recontextualizes an action. A full sleep-and-wake nightmare scene requires its own dramatic turn.
- Clarity rule: G003 must separately record presentation order, story-time position, viewpoint or transition owner, and causal predecessors. By the final act, a viewer must be able to reconstruct the consequential chronology without an explanatory monologue.
- Evidence/classification: User-directed original structure using chaptered nonlinear, braided-ensemble, delayed-context, and dramatic-irony techniques; no claim that this presentation derives from an ancient source.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 treatment, beat sheet, character arcs, and scene index; G004 temporal and transition grammar; G005 screenplay; G006 story-time continuity states; G007 scene/shot inheritance; G010 animatic.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-032 — Open mid-service; the family catastrophe is withheld and alluded to

- Date: 2026-10-23.
- Supersedes: None; refines AD-024, AD-025, and AD-031.
- Decision: The feature does not open with Hercules's life with Megara or the madness. It opens with Hercules already in service, mid-Labor. The family's deaths enter through allusion and indirection: characters ask why he serves, where his wife is, or what he is atoning for; objects, absences, reactions, and partial memory supply the rest. Dialogue need not state events literally.
- Disclosure rule: Each allusion must change what the audience or a character understands, and the full causal picture is assembled progressively. The chronology of AD-001 is unchanged: madness precedes service, and the pre-madness life with Megara is shown only where a displaced scene earns it under AD-031.
- Representation rule: The killings remain unmistakable by the time they are fully presented (AD-024), but implied, fractured-subjective, or silhouetted treatment is the default. Direct depiction is allowed only where it serves the narrative-purpose test. Staging should not depend on graphic imagery to land.
- Evidence/classification: User-directed original structure; the in-medias-res reveal is a presentation choice with no ancient source claim.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 opening image, beat sheet, and disclosure map; G004 violence ladder; G005 screenplay; G006 memory-state assets; G007 shot planning.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-033 — Dark comedy with consequence

- Date: 2026-10-23.
- Supersedes: None; refines AD-024 and clarifies AD-010; replaces "adult mythic tragedy" in the project's tone statement.
- Decision: The film is a dark comedy with real consequence, not a straight tragedy. Humor arises from character and situation, as a way people cope, and is not governed by rules about who may joke or when. Prometheus greeting the eagle ("Good morning, old friend") is the model. Hercules is earnest and does not joke about the gods.
- Boundary: AD-024's rating target and its prohibition on humor at a victim's expense remain. Eurystheus may be funny and remains dangerous.
- Evidence/classification: User-directed original tone choice.
- Affected downstream artifacts: `PROJECT.md`; G003 principles and treatment; G004 tone rules; G005 screenplay.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-034 — The gods are careless, address the audience, and are not strictly invisible

- Date: 2026-10-23.
- Supersedes: None; refines AD-026, AD-027, and AD-028.
- Decision: The gods are careless people, in the manner of Tom and Daisy Buchanan, who damage mortal lives and retreat into their position. Their persecution of Hercules is persistent but unreflective. They may speak to the audience in a documentary-like manner, commenting on scenes. Their commentary is partial, biased, and sometimes wrong, and it is not an exposition channel. Gods are not strictly invisible to mortals; exceptions are allowed when they earn a purpose.
- Boundary: Olympus remains interpenetrating (AD-026): divine scenes are anchored in mortal sets, and mortal objects and events cross into Olympus. Zeus's passivity is still a culpable choice (AD-027).
- Evidence/classification: User-directed original framing device.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 divine arcs and hinge map; G004 Olympus grammar; G005 screenplay.
- Status: Approved.

### AD-035 — Simultaneous world: the day starts everywhere

- Date: 2026-10-23.
- Supersedes: None; extends AD-029 and AD-031.
- Decision: Distant figures (Prometheus and the eagle, Atlas, Cerberus, Theseus and Pirithous, Hera, and others) are woven into ordinary scenes at varying levels of attention: foreground, peripheral, or short montage. Recurring dawn passages show the world starting its day in many places at once. Prometheus's daily eagle is the film's clock. Simultaneity is preferred to flashback or flash-forward; any true displacement needs a hinge visible within the story and must earn its place.
- Production note: Dense multi-layer tableaux should be planned as layered plates for G004 and G007, not single-prompt shots.
- Evidence/classification: User-directed original structure.
- Affected downstream artifacts: G003 outline, scene index (ambient threads), and hinge map; G004 temporal grammar; G007 layered scene packages.
- Status: Approved.
