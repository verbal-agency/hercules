# Adaptation Decision Log

Status: Approved for G003 treatment development on 2026-08-08

Changes to an approved decision require a new dated entry; do not rewrite the historical rationale invisibly.

## Story canon

### AD-001 — Madness precedes the Labors

- Decision: Use Pseudo-Apollodorus's chronology. Hera causes a catastrophic break after Hercules has built a home with Megara; the Labors follow as legal/religious purification and become chosen atonement.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.12. Euripides's post-Labor chronology (SRC_A03) is documented but not combined with it.
- Rationale: This creates the feature's causal spine and makes strength morally insufficient.

### AD-002 — The dead are not reversible plot tokens

- Decision: Megara and the children die. No resurrection, secret survival, or revelation that the deaths were an illusion.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.12 and SRC_A03.
- Rationale: Atonement requires an irreparable loss. The film must not imply that completing tasks purchases forgiveness from the dead.

### AD-003 — Responsibility is shared but not erased

- Decision: Hera deliberately precipitates the madness; Hercules is not morally guilty in the ordinary intentional sense, yet he accepts responsibility for living after harm done through his body and power.
- Rationale: Avoid both victim-blaming and a consequence-free possession story.

### AD-004 — Ten tasks become twelve

- Decision: Eurystheus announces ten, then rejects the Hydra because of Iolaus's help and the Stables because of promised payment.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.5.1–12.
- Rationale: The shifting count embodies institutional cruelty and tests whether Hercules serves only for acquittal.

### AD-005 — The Labor arc moves from force toward restraint

- Decision: G003 must organize the selected Labor material so Hercules increasingly solves problems through patience, cooperation, mercy, ingenuity, and endurance.
- Classification: Original dramatic organization grounded in ancient episodes.
- Rationale: Repetition becomes character progression rather than twelve interchangeable victories.

### AD-006 — Completion is not absolution

- Decision: Returning Cerberus ends compulsory service, not grief. Hercules's climax is choosing continued life and relational responsibility.
- Basis: Original resolution influenced by Theseus stopping suicide in SRC_A03 1214–1428.

### AD-007 — The first feature ends after Cerberus

- Decision: End with service complete, Cerberus restored alive, and Hercules stepping into a self-directed life. Deianeira, Nessus, Iole, the poisoned robe, pyre, and apotheosis are neither dramatized nor teased in a mandatory coda.
- Basis: The reserved cycle is SRC_A01 2.7.5–7, SRC_A04, and SRC_A02 4.36–39.
- Rationale: Preserves a complete atonement film and protects a later tragedy from summary treatment.

## Character functions

### AD-008 — Megara is the human center of the lost life

- Decision: Megara is Hercules's adult partner and moral equal, not a reward or disposable motivation. Her agency, family relationships, and perception of Hercules must be established before the catastrophe. She does not appear as a recurring ghost who forgives him.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.11–12; the expanded threatened family in SRC_A03.

### AD-009 — Iolaus makes interdependence unavoidable

- Decision: Iolaus is Hercules's nephew, companion, driver, and practical counterweight. His indispensable Hydra aid is real even though Eurystheus weaponizes it to reject the Labor.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.11, 2.5.2.

### AD-010 — Eurystheus is frightened power, not comic relief

- Decision: Eurystheus is a legitimate king produced by Hera's birth-order intervention. He is fearful, status-conscious, and procedurally cruel; brief vessel-hiding humor is allowed, but he remains dangerous.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.5, 2.5.1; SRC_A05 19.95–133.

### AD-011 — Hera is the divine antagonist, not a wronged-wife caricature

- Decision: Hera targets Zeus's visible exception to divine and civic order. Her persecution is intentional and unjust; the film does not excuse it as ordinary jealousy or make Alcmene/Megara responsible for Zeus's acts.
- Basis: SRC_A01, SRC_A05, SRC_A09.

### AD-012 — Athena aids without solving

- Decision: Athena supplies bounded tools, knowledge, or openings—especially the Stymphalian clappers—and may protect the infant, but Hercules and companions must choose and act.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.4.8, 2.5.6; SRC_A05 8.362–69; REF_0002.

### AD-013 — Theseus embodies survival through solidarity

- Decision: Hercules frees Theseus during the Cerberus descent. Their relationship, seeded no later than G003, lets Theseus recognize and answer Hercules's isolation. Pirithous remains bound.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.5.12, SRC_A12; thematic influence from SRC_A03.

### AD-014 — Zeus is absent power with consequences

- Decision: Zeus does not descend to settle the conflict or apologize. His past actions create Hercules's predicament; his silence keeps the human cost visible.
- Basis: Ancient traditions consistently establish paternity but differ on divine involvement.

### AD-015 — The gods use bounded manifestations

- Decision: Hera, Athena, Hermes, Artemis, Apollo, Hades, and Persephone may appear when selected episodes require them. Divine action must have legible rules and cannot reverse the central loss.
- Rationale: Keeps mythic scale without repeated deus ex machina.

## Tone and representation

### AD-016 — Audience boundary

- Decision: Aim for a family-accessible teen audience, approximately a modern PG-13 boundary: emotionally serious, frightening at times, and intelligible to younger viewers with adults, but never graphic.
- Violence rules: no visible killing of children; no lingering bodies; no gore; creature combat may be intense but prioritizes silhouette, consequence, and reaction; sexual violence is not depicted; suicide ideation may be expressed but not instructional or romanticized.
- Humor rules: humor releases pressure through character, scale, effort, and Eurystheus's fear. It may not mock grief, disability, foreign peoples, Amazons, or the victims of violence.
- Rationale: Resolves the carried G002 audience risk consistently with `PROJECT.md`.

### AD-017 — Madness is subjective and non-diagnostic

- Decision: Depict the event as divinely induced perceptual rupture, not as a named real-world mental illness. Avoid “madness equals evil,” asylum imagery, and stigmatizing shorthand.
- Basis: Personified Lyssa in SRC_A03 and Hera's agency in SRC_A01.

### AD-018 — Hippolyta's tragedy is caused by disinformation

- Decision: Hippolyta initially consents to give the belt. Hera's false report and Hercules's readiness to perceive betrayal turn agreement into fatal conflict.
- Basis: SRC_A01 2.5.9.
- Rationale: Gives the Amazon episode moral consequence and avoids conquest-as-default.

### AD-019 — Cultural and visual period boundary

- Decision: The story world is mythic Greek rather than a literal archaeological year. G004 may synthesize Late Bronze Age massing and technologies with Archaic/Classical iconographic clarity, but must not use Roman imperial Hercules as the unmarked default.
- Rules: Greek social vocabulary and names govern; no pop-medieval fantasy; no claim of archaeological reconstruction; geographic cultures must not become interchangeable “barbarian” caricatures.

### AD-020 — Naming policy

- Decision: The film title and English dialogue use `Hercules`; research uses `Heracles` for Greek-source precision. Greek forms govern other names (`Megara`, `Iolaus`, `Eurystheus`, `Athena`, `Heracles` in quoted scholarship), with familiar English spellings.
- Rationale: Audience recognition without silently Romanizing the whole world.

## Invention policy

### AD-021 — Permitted invention

- Decision: Original connective events, dialogue, relationship development, geography compression, and Labor intercutting are allowed when labeled in treatment notes and consistent with approved outcomes.
- Required test: invention must clarify causality, character, or production feasibility and may not be presented as ancient testimony.

### AD-022 — Prohibited flattening

- Decision: Do not merge contradictory variants merely to accumulate famous details. Select one outcome, record alternatives, and identify thematic borrowing across variants.

### AD-023 — Labor compression

- Decision: All twelve Labors exist in story canon, but G003 may allocate full sequences, short sequences, montage treatment, or off-screen completion. Each must retain its selected outcome and contribution to the character arc.
- Rationale: A 105–115 minute feature cannot give twelve equal set pieces and still sustain drama.

Append a new decision with date, superseded ID, evidence, affected downstream artifacts, and approval status. Approved source history must remain readable.
