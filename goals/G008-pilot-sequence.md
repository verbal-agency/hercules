# G008 — Representative Sequence Pilot

Status: Pending

## Objective

Generate and edit one difficult representative sequence to determine whether scene inheritance, shot-level control, asset pairing, and model compilation can sustain narrative clarity, claymation style, and visual continuity.

## Dependencies

G007.

## Project scenarios advanced

PS-03, PS-04, PS-05, PS-08.

## Constraints

- Select the pilot by coverage of risk, not convenience.
- Do not expand to full production until pilot exit criteria pass.
- Preserve every generation run and evaluation result.
- Defer Higgsfield account creation, authentication, and paid experimentation until G006 has approved assets and G007 has produced an auditable pilot package; do not make provider access a dependency of earlier story or visual-development goals.

## In scope

- Pilot selection, complete scene and child-shot packages, compiled generation requests, Higgsfield setup, comparative model runs, generation, editorial assembly, continuity evaluation, credit accounting, failure taxonomy, and architecture revisions required by findings.

## Exclusions

- Full-feature generation package.

## Deliverables

- Pilot brief and selection rationale.
- Schema-valid pilot scene and shot packages with resolved asset inputs.
- Compiled model requests for every pilot shot.
- Generation logs and selects.
- Edited pilot sequence.
- Evaluation report and go/no-go decision.
- Higgsfield provider evaluation and export/reproducibility report.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The pilot tests character continuity, creature motion, environmental continuity, effects, and editorial coverage.
- [ ] The pilot includes a hinge (a cross-world or cross-time transition) and either a layered-plate tableau or a documented reason it is deferred.
- [ ] Provider handling of the violent imagery the story requires is tested early with staging that works as implied or fractured imagery, and the result is recorded.
- [ ] Every generated shot is traceable through its scene package, shot package, resolved assets, compiled request, model settings, and source output.
- [ ] Cross-shot invariants are inherited from the scene package rather than duplicated inconsistently across shot prompts.
- [ ] The pilot completes every comparison required by `platforms/HIGGSFIELD.md` or documents why a comparison is unavailable.
- [ ] A location three-quarter master and reverse preserve the approved anchor, openings, light side, materials, palette, and depth.
- [ ] Manual setup and AI Director outputs are compared without allowing either to overwrite canonical decisions silently.
- [ ] At least two viable underlying models are evaluated against the same approved intent where their controls permit a fair comparison.
- [ ] Estimated and actual credits are recorded per run and summarized per accepted second.
- [ ] Repository records can reconstruct or audit each selected Higgsfield run without treating the Higgsfield workspace as the sole source of truth.
- [ ] The assembled sequence communicates its story beat without explanatory text.
- [ ] Predefined continuity and claymation-style thresholds pass, or the goal remains incomplete pending an approved change.
- [ ] Material failures are classified as story, asset, package, model, or editorial problems.
- [ ] Required systemic changes are implemented and reverified before a go decision.

## Expected verification evidence

- Pilot playback review against a rubric established before generation.
- Automated traceability and package validation.
- Before/after evidence for corrected systemic failures.
- Provider metadata/export audit and credit reconciliation.

## Carried risk

- The quality threshold must be agreed before generation to avoid moving the target after seeing results.
- Provider onboarding and credit commitment remain deliberately deferred until the pilot has approved asset inputs and explicit experiment scope.
