# G010 — Animatic and Production Handoff

Status: Pending

## Objective

Assemble a timed feature animatic and deliver a verified production handoff identifying coverage, cost, quality, continuity, and unresolved risk.

## Dependencies

G009.

## Project scenarios advanced

PS-02, PS-03, PS-04, PS-07, PS-08.

## Constraints

- The animatic may use boards, pilot footage, and approved placeholders.
- Placeholder status must remain explicit.
- Do not misrepresent the animatic as a final film.

## In scope

- Timed assembly, placeholder coverage, pacing review, continuity review, generation plan, risk register, and handoff index.

## Exclusions

- Final film generation, final sound mix, score licensing, distribution, and marketing.

## Deliverables

- Feature animatic.
- Editorial decision list or equivalent timeline inventory.
- Coverage report.
- Production plan and prioritized risk register.
- Handoff index linking story, assets, scene packages, shot packages, compiled requests, generations, and edit materials.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every screenplay scene is represented or explicitly approved as removed.
- [ ] Runtime and pacing are measured against the approved story architecture.
- [ ] Placeholder, pilot, generated-select, and missing media states are visually identifiable.
- [ ] Continuity review finds no unexplained canonical state changes.
- [ ] The production plan estimates generation volume and identifies the highest-risk sequences.
- [ ] A fresh collaborator can locate every authoritative artifact from the handoff index.
- [ ] All unresolved issues are assigned to the risk register or backlog with an owner or decision requirement.

## Expected verification evidence

- Full animatic playback and scene-coverage check.
- Timeline/media inventory audit.
- Handoff-link validation and fresh-reader recovery test.
