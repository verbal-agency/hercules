# Outline Draft (v4)

Status: Draft for discussion. Not approved; not a G003 deliverable. Governed by AD-032 to AD-035. Durations are targets (about 111 minutes).

## Conventions

- **Clocks:** each thread keeps its own clock at its own speed, and the film cross-references them. A transition needs a time marker only when time matters.
  - **Eagle (daily):** Zeus's eagle lands on Prometheus each dawn. True dawns are rare: the opening, the year of the Hind, and the dawn the eagle does not come.
  - **Sky (seasonal):** Atlas's sky sags and the stars drift. Weather is its symptom.
  - **Ledger (plot):** Eurystheus's chalk tally of Labors, ten becoming twelve.
  - **Olympus (episodic):** varied scenes that show the gods' state, such as the morning after a party or a chase.
- **Hinge:** the visible link between two scenes (shared line, object, animal carrier, contradiction, divine guide, or a character's exit or entrance).
- **Owner:** whose position opens the movement.
- Hermes may do the morning rounds, but not every time.

## Threads

- **Labor purposes:** Delphi (2, 9), political containment (2, 3, L1), Hera's attempt (2, 4, 9), the world's need (7, 11), his own responsibility (12 to 14).
- **Nightmare progression:** denial (5), accusation plus Abderus (8), false perception (10), remembering without absolution (13 to 14).
- **Prometheus:** D1, D3, the rock in 3, freed in 12, absent eagle in D6.
- **Atlas:** D1, A1, then Apples.
- **Rhymes:** help rejected (3) and help accepted (13); false perception (9, 10); the stolen cattle of Olympus (O2) and Geryon's cattle (11).
