## What the panel got wrong or padded

- **False positive on canon.** Three reviewers flagged "twelve years" against "ten labors" as an inconsistency. Apollodorus has Delphi assign twelve years of service and ten labors, so the scene was consistent with the source. Reviewer claims about the source tradition must be checked, not accepted.
- **Disagreement on the grief joke.** The tone reader and canon auditor treated it as a violation to cut. The cold audience, story analyst, and comedy writer called it a strong dark premise that needed a visible reaction from Hercules. That is a genuine taste split. Record it and let the user decide; do not average.
- **Padding.** Every reviewer produced three to five findings even on scene Y, including Low items that read as a quota. The output format should permit "no findings."
- **Convergence.** The cold audience, story analyst, and comedy writer overlapped heavily on exposition. Lens separation is imperfect.

## Useful notes the panel produced on scene Y

These are real input for the Eurystheus chapter, not just calibration:

1. Hercules's only want ("And then I'm done?") is thin, and nobody answers it. Let the denial be dramatized.
2. "Write that the next task is to be harder" and "Then write that it is harder than that" spend the same joke twice.
3. Copreus has no distinct voice.
4. The king counting on his fingers cannot be seen from inside the jar.
5. "The court steps back all at once" implies an unconstrained crowd. Name three or four courtiers.
6. The task count could be planted as fragile (a margin note on the tablet) so the later jump to twelve reads as seeded.
7. Open question from three readers: why does Hercules obey a frightened man? This may be a deliberate withhold under AD-032.

## Limits of this calibration

- One scene pair; the defects were blatant and easy.
- All reviewers are the same model family.
- Not yet tested: subtle defects, a good scene against a better one, macro reviews, a human table read.
- Conclusion: the panel reliably flags blatant failures and separates a flat scene from a working one. It has not shown it can distinguish good writing from great writing, and should not be used to approve anything.
