## Findings shared by several lenses

1. **Chapters 10 to 12 (Geryon, Prometheus, Atlas) read as "a labor, solved differently."** Raised by four lenses. No stated cost, no bearing on the king's goalposts or the withheld family story. Atlas was named as the least damaging cut. The Prometheus chapter was praised in tone but is also planted nowhere earlier.
2. **The void-the-labor mechanism repeats** (Hydra, then Stables). Raised by four lenses. It escalates the count but not the form.
3. **The ending is unresolved where the film most needs payoff.** Raised by the cold reader, story analyst, and energy rater. This is the known [open] thirteenth task. The cold reader also found "chooses to keep living" is an idea, not an image.
4. **Birds and Bull is a dull transit chapter** (cold reader, energy rater, story analyst).
5. **Plants with no payoff** (ledger keeper, with cold reader on Iolaus):
   - the thumbprint in chapter 1 never returns;
   - the eagle "clock" has no appearance before chapter 11;
   - the "smell" and the house he can't enter drop after chapter 7;
   - the legend thread and Hera's consequence have no carrying chapter;
   - Iolaus has no payoff after chapter 2;
   - "the debt was never the king's to collect" is not planted before chapter 14.

## Heavy-hand findings

The detector pointed at synopsis lines that state their own meaning: "Force has made things worse", "having taken the wrong lesson from the Hydra", "so it accuses", "He is not wiser or absolved", and "After this chapter the jokes stop around her and return everywhere else". It also said the help arc (chapters 2, 6, 13) is a visible three-step lesson, and that the mechanisms are repeated enough to read as formula.

Caveat: these lines are in the synopsis's explanatory voice, which is normal for a synopsis. A screenplay need not carry them. Discount the severity accordingly, but treat the structural point (the help arc is visible as a lesson) as real.

## Disagreement

The help arc. The story analyst and ledger keeper praised it as a clean setup and payoff (planted in chapter 2, tested in 6, reversed in 13). The heavy-hand detector read the same arc as a lesson being stored and paid off. Both can be true. This is a taste decision for the user.

## What worked, across lenses

The jar and the king ruling from inside it; the thumbprint as character without a speech; "Good morning, old friend"; the fragment growing each time it returns; the placement of the Megara chapter.

## Limits of this review

A synopsis cannot show whether dialogue is funny or whether scenes are alive. The energy rater said so and rated its own confidence moderate-low. All reviewers share a model family. The cold reader is not a real cold reader. These notes show where a pitch loses a listener, not whether the film works.
